## What the workflow proves

Both physical hosts executed a durable-state harness. After one Runner.Listener was terminated, the other host reclaimed two GitHub-artifact-transferred JSON task records, advanced epoch 1→2, rejected stale completion claims, and preserved a checkpoint reference. The returning host then executed a rejoin job under a new listener identity.

This is **not complete native Broker/Worker workload migration**. In `goriq-two-node-failover-e2e.ts:200–244`, the survivor directly calls `completeClaimed` with a constructed result. Its checkpoint is a reference string. Actual signed results, checkpoint payload restoration, independent workload continuity, PINNED-only waiting, SIDE_EFFECTING deduplication, resync, conflict resolution and rebalance remain unverified. Coordinator loss, network partition, OS reboot and AC/power recovery were not tested here.

The [raw receipt](./1745-node-loss-final-raw.json) is preserved byte-for-byte. Its `watchdogRecovered` and `watchdogAutomaticRecoveryProven` flags are assigned by the v1 verifier after identity/status checks; the verifier does not independently prove launch causality or absence of intervention. Missing `consecutiveRunnerFailures` defaults to zero. The receipt's `recoveredAt` is receipt creation time, not first recovery. These historical fields must not be promoted into broader product acceptance.
